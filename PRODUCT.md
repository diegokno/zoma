# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who own a physical Soma cube and want a quick source of new figures to build. They typically use the app beside the puzzle on a phone or tablet, choose a challenge, manipulate the physical pieces, and reveal the solution only when needed.

## Product Purpose

Provide exact, solvable Soma cube challenges without requiring an account or manual image production. Success means a player can immediately understand a figure, inspect it from every angle, attempt it physically, and reveal a trustworthy piece-by-piece solution.

## Brand Personality

Clear, playful, and precise. The experience should feel like a thoughtfully made tabletop-puzzle companion: approachable enough for casual use, but rigorous enough that every cube position can be trusted.

## Anti-references

Avoid childish game styling, noisy arcade interfaces, generic SaaS dashboards, decorative 3D effects, and interfaces that obscure the geometry. Do not use AI-generated challenge imagery or any representation that can alter the actual cube structure.

## Design Principles

- Geometry is the source of truth: challenge and solution must come from the same coordinates.
- Put the puzzle first: controls stay quiet and the figure receives most of the screen.
- Make inspection effortless: rotation, reset, challenge switching, and solution reveal should be immediate.
- Earn trust through consistency: camera, lighting, scale, colors, and labels behave identically across figures.
- Start quickly: no account, onboarding flow, or explanatory landing page before the first challenge.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Never rely on color alone to distinguish pieces: combine color with piece labels and visible separation. Support keyboard controls, clear focus states, sufficient contrast, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, and reduced-motion preferences.
